- build/check/test operations are deterministic/repeatable against the same exact source and pinned inputs;
- generated/lock/provenance checks fail on drift rather than silently rewrite source;
- a moved master invalidates the integration attempt and requires reconciliation/requalification;
- a changed source candidate invalidates prior exact-head CI qualification;
- no force-push/ref rewrite is an accepted retry path;
- failed external CI is diagnosed and corrected, not blind-rerun-to-green.
