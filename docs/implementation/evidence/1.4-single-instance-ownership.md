## Behavior proven by the candidate

- One stable user-scoped global mutex is acquired with bounded handling for acquired, abandoned, timeout, and failure outcomes.
- Existing-owner handling validates fixed-root, object/path, owner-state, protected-SID ACL, and handle identity conditions before recovery or activation.
- The fixed `LocalAppData\JARVIS` layout contains `data`, `backups`, `logs`, `cache`, `artifacts`, `modules`, `updates`, and `recovery`.
- Owner state uses a bounded, checksummed dual-slot record with generation fencing and impossible-state rejection.
- Same-session activation is bounded and succeeds only after readiness, exact generation/request identity, callback success, acknowledged state, and acknowledgement signalling are proven.
- Tauri qualification records the final owner snapshot only after a positive live-child observation; an exited owner fails before window inspection, while visibility and foreground ownership remain required.
- Normal and maintenance roles arbitrate explicitly; role mismatch, stale/corrupt state, reparse/UNC/alternate-root conditions, and changed identities fail closed.
- Close-before-release ordering, partial resource closure, repeated lifecycle calls, concurrent lifecycle operations, worker panic/failure, cancellation/release races, and recovery/restart paths are covered by the qualification suite.
- Tauri show/focus dispatch remains bounded and native; an unacknowledged second launch is a non-success result.
- Diagnostics and evidence are bounded, redacted, candidate-bound, and separately hash-bound to retained qualification logs.

These claims describe the tested Section 1.4 scope only. They do not claim completion of the later Core/runtime packaging, IPC, Job Object, provider, persistence, Mission Control, installer, signing, or release sections.
